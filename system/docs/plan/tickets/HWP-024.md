# Props, their frame logic, hit shake, and the creature's eyes

## Work

Per kind, with the asset list's frame counts: bush plays its two rustle frames for 24 ticks after `hitTick`; log plays its two chatter frames; rock steps through its four crack frames and stays on the last when `active`; flag plays its two raise frames then loops its two wave frames when `active`; beacon plays its two lighting frames then loops its two lit frames once active; torch, lantern and mushrooms loop whatever frames their strip has and are skipped when the manifest has no strip for them. A hit shakes the prop for four ticks. Two red eyes blink at the creature's hiding place and ride on the hat while carried; while travelling nothing is drawn.

## Acceptance

Charging each prop kind in the glade produces the expected animation and the eyes blink at the bush at start (screenshots). The eyes on the hat are checked in HWP-052, once the creature moves.
