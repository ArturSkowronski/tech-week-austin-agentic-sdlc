# The world heals per branch, and the card says the logs are clean

## Work

The game creates a creature only when `bugForRoom(room)` exists; with no bug the card shows "The logs are clean." after the water is parted and the waterfall shows flavour lines only.

## Acceptance and test

`bugs.test.ts`: `bugsIn([], room)` is empty and a world for a room with no bug has `creature.at === -1` forever. A private branch with an emptied manifest shows no eyes and the clean card in Playwright.
