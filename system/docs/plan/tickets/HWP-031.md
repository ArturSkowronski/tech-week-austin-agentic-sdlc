# Build the background layers into public/assets with manifest art rows

## Work

From the four raws in `assets/` of the plan (copied into `assets/`): mountains mirror-tiled to 640 wide with a 120-row base in `#7b9682`; tall forest with a 120-row base in `#213a2d`; undergrowth; the foreground as the top 76 rows of the undergrowth mirrored. Each reduced to 32 colours; art rows recorded in the manifest. Also the splash: `splash-textless.png` resampled at its pitch to `splash.png`, 320 by 180, 32 colours, for the title screen. `build.ts` is the kit's entry point; repoint the `assets` script at it.

## Acceptance

`public/assets/bg/*.png` match the sizes in the asset list; `splash.png` is 320 by 180; the manifest carries `artRows`; `pnpm assets` twice changes no bytes.
