# Art: the cliff ruins panel and the stone face landmark, traced

## Human

Generate the panel and the stone face as a separate landmark image, record provenance, open a PR with the raws.

## Visdom

Build, place the face as a mid-layer landmark (a new kit output kind, `landmarks/`, built by `landmarks.ts` and drawn by `scene.ts`; a room names its landmark and that counts as its consumer), trace the ledges, place the rock, the lanterns (`N`, art from HWP-043) and the beacon, add the playthrough.

## Acceptance

A fresh player reaches the summit beacon; drops between islands are recoverable to a flag; the playthrough passes.
