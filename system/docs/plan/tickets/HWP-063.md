# Sound effects from simulation events and a music loop behind a toggle

## Human

Generate the thirteen effects and the music loop with the audio prompts; record provenance; confirm the music licence tier.

## Visdom

The kit copies audio into `public/assets/audio/` with manifest entries and the names go on the consumer list in `sprites.ts`; `audio.ts` plays a sound per `state.events` entry, never from render timing; a settings toggle for music, remembered in `localStorage`.

## Acceptance

In a replay, the sequence of played sounds equals the sequence of events (unit test on the mapping); music starts only after the toggle and a user gesture.
