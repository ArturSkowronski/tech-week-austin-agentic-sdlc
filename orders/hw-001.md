and the creature jumps onto the hat. From then on the keys feel swapped: the right arrow key walks the
wizard left and the left arrow key walks him right. The keyboard is fine on the title screen and
before the creature climbs on.

Steps:
1. Walk through the bush; the creature jumps on.
2. Hold the right arrow key.

Criteria:
- with the creature on the hat, holding right walks the wizard right, and holding left walks him left
- jump and charge work the same with or without the creature
