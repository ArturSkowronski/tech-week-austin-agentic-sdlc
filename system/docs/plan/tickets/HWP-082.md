# Ten trial visitors complete the loop unassisted

## Work

Ten people outside the team play the game at its production URL, meet the creature, read the logs, raise a ticket, and play the fixed preview, with no help. Record time to first creature, time to ticket, and blockers. Open follow-up tickets for every blocker.

## Acceptance

10 of 10 complete the loop — bug found, ticket raised, fix PR with a preview played; the metrics in the visitor journey are met or a ticket exists for each miss.
