# Water current, pull, drag, death at the bottom, reveal stamp

## Work

`water.ts`: overlap of the player box with a waterfall prop's column sets downward speed to at least 160, multiplies horizontal speed by 0.85, forbids jumping, ends a charge, and stamps the prop's `hitTick`. Reaching the room's bottom row while in water is a death.

## Acceptance and test

`water.test.ts`: a wizard dropped into the column reaches the bottom and dies within 60 ticks; jump input in water does not change vertical speed; a charge entering water ends on that tick; `hitTick` equals the tick of first contact; a jump-then-charge from the plateau clears the glade's six-tile gap without touching water, and a plain full jump from the plateau does not reach the far side.
