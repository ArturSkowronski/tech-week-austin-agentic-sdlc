# The waterfall of logs drawn in code

## Work

A dark rock face with a rounded right edge; log text columns falling at 0.9 to 1.6 units per tick from the manifest's lines for the room's bug; the water on top as a base blue with thin scrolling strands of a second blue, streaks in fixed columns at 3 to 5 units per tick, foam along the lip and the right edge, mist at the base; near opaque with faint green flickers until `hitTick` is within 150 ticks, then parted. Only drawn when on screen. Everything driven by `state.tick`, never by wall time.

## Acceptance

Screenshots before and after a charge hit show closed and parted water; the log lines are legible when parted at scale 4; frame output is identical for identical ticks.
