# Camera and the parallax layer stack with the sky gradient

## Work

- `camera.ts`: dead zone, clamp to the room, round last.
- The four background layers with the table's horizontal and vertical factors and bottom offsets, mountains mirror-tiled, tints and alpha as specified; the fill below the horizon in the mountains' base colour; the sky as bands of 6 units between the three dusk stops, redrawn only when the horizon moves.
- `sprites.ts`: density-aware loaders reading `public/assets/manifest.json`.

## Acceptance

Screenshots at rest and at the top of the ledge climb show the trunks and gold light at rest and the canopy and peaks when climbed, with no pale gap between layers. Camera delta is zero when clamped at the room edge (a Playwright step with the player against the right wall shows an unchanged frame).
