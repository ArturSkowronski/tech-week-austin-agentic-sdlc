# Room parser, props, flags, beacon, spikes, deaths and respawn

## Work

- `room.ts`: `parseRoom(text)` for the whole legend of the level design doc, including the waterfall anchored by its surface tile and the trigger-less `N` and `M` lights; `PROP_SIZE` as the single source of prop cell sizes; props placed bottom on the tile below; spawn with feet on the tile below `P`.
- `props.ts`: flag raises on overlap and becomes the checkpoint; beacon overlap sets `victoryTick` and `victory` (HWP-046 later makes this the hand-over to the next room for every room but the last); rock opens on a charge hit.
- `checkpoint.ts`: tile-to-world conversion for the respawn point (the future home of HW-002; keep the conversion in one function).
- Spikes, water bottom and leaving the room kill: `deaths` increments, the wizard respawns at the checkpoint with `hurt` invulnerability.
- `game/content/rooms/index.ts` as a glob over the room modules only; `forest-glade.ts` and `ruin-gate.ts` from the reference maps in [rooms/](../02-design/rooms/).

## Acceptance and test

`world.test.ts` grows: the glade parses to 80 by 22 with the expected prop kinds and count; spawn is at tile (2, 17) with feet on row 18; touching a flag then dying respawns at the checkpoint rather than the spawn (the position to the unit is the paired test of HW-002's, not this suite's); the beacon sets `victoryTick`; the ruin gate parses to 132 by 22.
