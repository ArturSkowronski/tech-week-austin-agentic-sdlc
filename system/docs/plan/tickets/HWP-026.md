# Ticket card, completion banner, title screen and HUD line

## Work

- `panel.ts`: shared chrome for the card and the banner, drawn in code, full-resolution text.
- `ticket.ts`: the four card states from the bug-quest doc, the button, dismissable after three seconds, T key callback.
- `title.ts`: the splash with a code-drawn wordmark; Enter, Space or Z starts; Enter never restarts a run.
- `hud.ts`: room name and the debug state label behind F1.
- Banner: time from `victoryTick` and deaths.

## Acceptance

Text is crisp at 1280 by 720 and at 1920 by 1080; the card cannot be dismissed before three seconds and can after; Enter during a run does nothing.
