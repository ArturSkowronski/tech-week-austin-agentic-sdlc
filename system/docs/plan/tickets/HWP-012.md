# Player locomotion, run and variable jump with coyote time and jump buffer

The wizard walks and jumps; there is no bounce and no charged jump (ADR-002).

## Work

- `player.ts`: the state table for `idle, run, jump, fall, land` with `stateTimer`; ground and air acceleration; gravity and terminal velocity; jump velocity, jump cut, coyote, buffer; `fellFor` and the long-fall threshold; facing.

## Acceptance and test

`jump.test.ts`: a held jump from flat ground peaks between 52 and 56 units above the start (about three and a third tiles); a jump released after 4 ticks peaks lower than 30 units; pressing jump 5 ticks after walking off a ledge still jumps, 7 ticks does not; jump pressed 5 ticks before landing jumps on the landing tick. `fall.test.ts`: a drop of four tiles sets `fellFor` at or above 20; a full jump on flat ground never does.
