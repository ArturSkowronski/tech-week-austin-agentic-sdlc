# The wand charge, dash with gravity off, wall recoil, cooldown, hitbox

## Work

- States `charge` and `chargeRecoil`: 2-tick wind-up, 14 ticks at 260 in the facing direction with gravity off, then fall or idle; a hit on a solid tile enters recoil for 3 ticks; cooldown 24 ticks; no vulnerability during the charge.
- `charge-hit.ts`: the 12 by 8 hitbox at the crystal tip, leading the body, swept along the dash; overlap with a prop stamps its `hitTick` and emits an event. This module is the future home of HW-003, so it must contain the sweep in one obvious place.

## Acceptance and test

`charge.test.ts`: a charge covers about 60 units on flat ground; vertical speed stays zero during it; a charge from full run speed into a one-tile wall ends in `chargeRecoil` on the near side of the wall; pressing attack within 24 ticks of a charge does nothing; a charge through a bush stamps the bush's `hitTick` to the current tick.
