# Density 2 from the raws we have: props, tiles and the wizard sheets

No new art. The raws in `assets/` hold more detail than the game shows: the prop sheet draws a bush 204 px wide and the kit shrinks it to a 32 px cell, the tileset draws 205 px cells and the kit shrinks them to 16 px, and the renderer then doubles every pixel back up (ADR-003). Shrinking to two pixels per unit instead of one keeps the detail and costs nothing at run time, because the renderer already scales by the manifest's density.

## Work

- `props.ts`: cut every strip at 2 px per unit (a 32 by 24 bush becomes a 64 by 48 cell) and write `density: 2`. Cell sizes in world units do not change; `PROP_SIZE` stays the one source.
- `tiles.ts`: resample each tile to 32 px and write `density: 2`; `game/render/tiles.ts` cuts the atlas by the manifest's density rather than a fixed 16.
- `strips.ts`: the wizard sheets were drawn at 6 to 10 px blocks, so they hold roughly 1x drawing, but their outlines and shading are finer than the blocks. Resample each pose to a 64 px cell (area average, or block sampling at half the recorded pitch) and write `density: 2`, then compare a screenshot against the 1x build. Keep whichever reads better per sheet; if a sheet gains nothing, leave it at density 1 and say so in the PR.
- The crystal position, the foot offset and every cell size stay in world units; only the pixel counts change. `estimatePitch` and `resampleBlocks` gain whatever parameter this needs.
- `asset-list.md`: statuses move from "1x done" to "density 2 from the 1x raw".

## Acceptance

Every prop, tile and wizard frame renders at the same world size as before with twice the pixels; `pnpm assets` is still idempotent and the consumer gate passes; F1 debug tiles still align with the painting-less room; a replay shows identical frames twice; the PR carries before and after screenshots of the glade's plateau (bush, log, flag) and of the wizard idle and run.
