# Tile atlas from the tileset grid and prop strips from the prop sheet

## Work

- `tiles.ts`: slice `tileset.png` by its known grid (205 by 202 cells from (39, 39) with 39 px gaps, then the plank and the spike trap) into 16 px tiles in the shared tile order.
- `props.ts`: find objects on `props-sheet.png` as connected regions through the alpha channel, group them into bands by top edge, and cut the seven strips (bush, log, rock, flag, beacon, torch, sparks) through a hand-written mapping of band and position that splits the touching beacons and the touching rocks into five columns each; cells from the simulation's `PROP_SIZE`, sparks 24 by 8, one PNG per prop; fail loudly when a band holds fewer objects than the mapping needs.

## Acceptance and test

`kit.test.ts` on fixture sheets: the tile slicer yields eleven tiles in order; the prop mapping yields the seven strips with the asset list's frame counts (bush 3, log 3, rock 5, flag 5, beacon 5, torch 1, sparks 5). The real sheets build without error.
