# The seed, main and stable branches with their domains

## Work

Create `seed` from the commit that plants every bug of the season and `stable` from `main`; add custom domains `play.`, `main.` and `stable.` routes to the Worker; document the reseeding procedure for a season in `docs/`.

## Acceptance

The three domains serve their branches; a visitor branching from `seed` meets the first creature, and each fix reveals the next.
