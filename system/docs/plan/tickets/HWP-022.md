# Tiles by context, the painting layer, and F1

## Work

- `tile-order.ts`: the atlas order, imported by the renderer and the kit.
- `tiles.ts`: pick a variant per solid tile from its neighbours: moss surface, corners at ledge ends, two rows of earth with scattered roots seeded from the tile position, stone below with edges and mossy blocks; plank for `-` and `=`; spike trap for `^`.
- When the room has a painting in the manifest, draw it at its density in place of the tiles; F1 toggles hitboxes and draws the tiles over the painting at half alpha. The ruin gate room module exists since M1 and its painting since HWP-034.

## Acceptance

The glade renders with corners at every ledge end and no visible seam; `?room=ruin-gate` shows the painting with no tiles until F1.
