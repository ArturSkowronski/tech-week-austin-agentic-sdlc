# The creature, hiding, riding, fleeing, letting go

## Work

`creature.ts` over `WorldState.creature`: starts at the manifest's hiding place kind (first prop of that kind in the room); walking through the prop sets `carriedSince` and `firstCarried`; a charge or 600 ticks drops it and it travels 30 ticks to the nearest hiding place; charging its hiding place makes it flee to another; a death drops it, after the respawn position has been computed. `createWorld`'s `creature` option enables it without the manifest for tests.

## Acceptance and test

`creature.test.ts`: the sequence above, tick-exact.
