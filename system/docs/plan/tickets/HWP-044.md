# The victory cast lights the beacon

Reaching the beacon plays a cast: the wizard spins, gathers a burst at the wand tip and fires a beam into the beacon, which lights as the beam lands. Then the banner. Today the beacon lights on touch and the wizard holds the idle loop.

The art exists: `assets/wizard-victory.png`, eight labelled poses generated on 2026-09-10 (idle, a spin over four, the burst, the beam, idle), with its provenance row, on the branch `art/wizard-victory` (PR #106, a draft that is not merged alone because the kit refuses an unread raw). Start by taking both files from it: `git fetch origin art/wizard-victory && git checkout FETCH_HEAD -- assets/wizard-victory.png assets/PROVENANCE.md`.

## Work

- **Kit.** Add the sheet to the wizard table in `build.ts`: state `victory`, 8 frames, the pitch that brings its poses (about 200 px tall in the raw) to the idle sheet's height, as the walk sheet's 7 does. The burst and the beam make poses 6 and 7 about 250 and 350 raw pixels wide, so the strip needs a 64-unit cell. The figure must not move when the cast starts: place every pose so the body, not the bounding box, sits where a 32-unit cell would put it, and give the manifest entry the anchor the renderer needs if that is not the cell's centre. The crystal finder picks the bluest cluster and would land on the swirl, the burst or the beam in poses 5 to 7; pin the crystal by hand for those frames in the sheet entry, or mark them as having no wand light. `sprites/wizard-victory` joins the consumer list.
- **Simulation.** Touching the beacon still sets `victoryTick`, stops the run clock and holds the wizard in `victory`, facing the beacon. The beacon itself lights later: `props.ts` sets its `active` and `hitTick` at `victoryTick + VICTORY_CAST_HIT_TICKS` and pushes a `beacon:lit` event then. The constant lives in `constants.ts` next to the other tick counts, together with `VICTORY_CAST_FRAME_TICKS`, the ticks each cast frame holds; the hit tick is the beam frame's first tick, derived from those two, so the two clocks cannot drift apart.
- **Renderer.** `victory` plays the eight frames once at `VICTORY_CAST_FRAME_TICKS` per frame and holds the last; the frame index comes from `stateTimer` and the same constants. The banner in `panel.ts` appears when the beacon is active, not when `victoryTick` is set.
- **Docs.** Level design's "The beacon ends the run", the `victoryTick` row of the simulation architecture, the beacon row of the state table in movement and physics, and the victory row of the asset list describe the cast.

## Acceptance

`victory.test.ts`: touching the beacon sets `victoryTick` and holds `victory`; the beacon is not active before `victoryTick + VICTORY_CAST_HIT_TICKS` and is active with a `beacon:lit` event on that tick; the run clock reads the touch tick. `world.test.ts` and the glade playthrough still pass. `wizard.test.ts`: the victory pose sequence is 1 to 8 once, then 8. In the browser, reaching the glade's beacon plays the cast, the beacon lights as the beam lands, the banner appears after, the wizard stays on his tile and the wand light stays on the wand; a replay shows identical frames twice. PR #106 is closed once this merges.
