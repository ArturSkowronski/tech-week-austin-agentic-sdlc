# Hobby Wizard scenes for the Austin deck

The art of the deck is the art of the game. The references are the game's own raws in
`system/assets/` (the title splash and the wand-charge sheet), so the wizard on the
slides is the wizard players meet in the forest. `gen.py` sends them with every prompt.

## Style lock

Detailed 16-bit pixel art in exactly the style of the attached Hobby Wizard title
screen: crisp square pixels, a limited saturated palette, dark one-pixel outlines,
dithered shading, no anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients. Deep night-blue sky with
small sparkling stars, mossy green forest, warm brown wood and stone, cyan magic
sparks from the crystal on the end of the wand.

The hero is the attached old wizard, kept exactly as in the references: tall floppy
green hat with a brown band and brass goggles on it, long grey beard, round nose,
green robe with gold trim and a brown belt, brown boots, a gnarled wooden wand with a
glowing blue crystal at the tip. He is busy and good-humoured, always doing something
specific, never posing. When the scene asks for "the bug", it is the small round
purple creature with two antennae and big white eyes from the splash's waterfall.

Composition reads like a game's level-intro card: one clear scene, a strong
silhouette, generous calm sky or wall at one side, readable at thumbnail size. The
factory machinery is built from the game's own materials: timber, stone, brass, rope,
moss, lanterns and glowing crystals, never modern steel or screens unless the scene
asks for one. No text, letters or numbers unless the scene asks for them.

## Negative

a second wizard, a crowd of people, a real-world person, any company logo or brand,
modern office furniture, photo-realism, 3D render, vector flat style, smooth
gradients, blur, painterly brush strokes, extra limbs, mangled hands, gibberish
lettering, watermarks, a frame or border around the image.

## cover

Scene: night in the forest, a small wooden factory built into a mossy hillside. A
conveyor of timber rollers comes out of its door carrying small glowing parchment
scrolls, each sealed with a cyan crystal. The wizard stands on a stone ledge in front,
wand raised like a conductor, sparks guiding the scrolls onto the belt. Above the
door three lanterns: one green, one amber, one dark. The waterfall from the splash on
the far right, the bug peeking out of it. Wide sky on the left two thirds.

## l01-agent

Scene: the wizard at a stone workbench carefully placing a small glowing golem made
of moss and stone into a niche in a wall of numbered stone niches, like a shelf of
cubbies; each niche has a brass plate and a little lantern. Several niches already
hold identical small golems with lit eyes. He checks a long parchment manifest held
in his other hand.

## l02-tools

Scene: a tool wall made of timber pegboard holding exactly five tools glowing softly:
a magnifying lens, a quill, a scroll, a small hammer and a little scale. The small
golem from the factory reaches for a sixth tool, a large key, which sits behind an
iron grate locked with a brass padlock; the wizard leans on the grate, arms crossed,
amused.

## l03-context

Scene: a library inside a hollow tree: shelves of scrolls sorted into five tiers
glowing in five different colours from the floor to the canopy. The wizard on a
rolling ladder hands one bound book down to the small golem waiting below. Roots and
moss around the shelves.

## l04-intake

Scene: a small wooden post-office window set into a stone wall. A crumpled, almost
empty parchment with only one scribble on it lies on the counter. The wizard behind
the window pushes it back out with his wand, three glowing question-mark sparks
floating above it. A neat queue of rolled scrolls with wax seals waits on a shelf
behind him.

## l05-line

Scene: a long production line of timber and brass stretching diagonally across the
frame in four stations: a sorting chute, a stamping press, a glowing anvil where the
small golem hammers, and a packing crate. A rope-and-pulley track carries a scroll
from station to station. The wizard walks along the line with a lantern, checking.

## l06-gates

Scene: three stone gates one after another across a forest path, each with a
different glowing rune lock (green, amber, red). A cart with a scroll goes through
the first two; the third gate has slammed down and stopped it, sparks flying. The
wizard peers through the bars of the third gate, nodding, pleased the gate held.

## l07-oracle

Scene: a hidden stone shrine behind the waterfall. Inside, a tall glowing crystal
oracle on a pedestal judges a scroll held up to it; a sealed iron chest with chains
(the vault) sits next to it, unopened. The small golem waits outside the waterfall
curtain, unable to see in; the wizard stands inside next to the oracle.

## l08-rework

Scene: a forest track that loops back on itself like a small roller-coaster made of
timber; a cart with a scroll goes around for the second time, a counter of three
stone beads above the loop with two beads lit red. At the end of the loop a big brass
alarm bell on a post, the wizard pulling its rope, red light spilling around.

## l09-lights

Scene: the factory at night seen from outside: three windows. The left window is
dark and quiet with machines working by themselves (lights-out); the middle window
glows amber with a scroll waiting on a desk under a bell jar; in the right window the
wizard signs a scroll with a quill under a warm lamp. A small queue of scrolls on a
rope line outside, only three hang, the rest wait in a basket.

## l10-ledger

Scene: an enormous ledger book on a stone lectern, its pages linked by a glowing
chain that runs from one page to the next. The wizard presses a large brass wax seal
with a crystal into the last page, cyan sparks. In the margin the bug tries to
scribble something; one chain link next to it has cracked and glows red.

## finale

Scene: the forest path from the splash with a wooden notice board on the right. A
player-made parchment ticket with a drawing of the bug is pinned to the board. The
wizard holds a glowing crystal jar with the bug safely caught inside, grinning, and
hands it towards a small golem carrying a neat rolled scroll tied with a green ribbon.

## close

Scene: dawn over the forest seen from the top of the stone tower from the splash.
The wizard sits on the tower's edge, legs dangling, with a mug, looking at the small
wooden factory in the valley below where some windows are lit and some are dark.
Large calm sky, warm first light on the mountains.
