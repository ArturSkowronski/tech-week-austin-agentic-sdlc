# Fireflies, motes, spores and mist seeded from the tick

## Work

Particle fields whose positions are pure functions of `state.tick` and a per-room seed: fireflies in the forest layer, motes in the wand light, spores at bushes, mist bands at the floor line and over water.

## Acceptance

Identical ticks render identical particles; 60 frames per second on a 2020 laptop with dev tools closed, measured with the Pixi ticker's FPS over ten seconds in Playwright.
