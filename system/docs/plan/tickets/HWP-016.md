# Scripted playthrough of the glade reaches the beacon

## Work

A recorded input script, written as segments (`hold(right, n)`, `press(jump)`, ...), that walks the glade from spawn to the beacon: the three ledges, the plateau, the jump-then-charge over the waterfall, the one-way climb over the spikes, past the rock, up to the beacon.

## Acceptance and test

`forest-room.test.ts`: the script ends with `victoryTick > 0` and `deaths === 0`, in fewer than 3600 ticks. The whole `tests/sim` suite runs in under one second.
