# One room: retire the ruin gate, HW-002 moves to the glade's log

Season 1 is one room, the glade ([ADR-009](../01-decisions/ADR-009-one-room.md)). The ruin gate was built as the second beat and is the only other room in the repository. This ticket removes it and re-plants its bug in the glade.

## Work

- Delete `game/content/rooms/ruin-gate.ts`, the two ruin gate raws and their rows in `assets/PROVENANCE.md` (list them under "Tried and set aside", kept outside the repository), and the ruin gate entry with its bridge and well cuts in `tools/asset-kit/paintings.ts`. The kit's painting step stays for a later room; with no raws it builds nothing, and the consumer gate must still pass. The map stays as `docs/plan/02-design/rooms/ruin-gate.txt` for reference.
- `bugs/manifest.json`: HW-002 gets `rooms: ["forest-glade"]` and hides in the `log` on the plateau; steps "Walk through the log; the creature jumps on." and "Raise the flag on the plateau, fall into the charge gap." The defect, its module, its logs and its paired test do not change; the paired test builds its own map and names no room.
- The glade shows one creature at a time: `bugForRoom` already returns the first entry for the room, so HW-001 hides in the bush until it is fixed and HW-002 appears in the log afterwards. Say so in the module comment of `game/sim/bugs.ts`.
- Delete the ruin gate playthrough and every test that names the room; the glade tests stay. `tests/sim/charge.test.ts` reserved the one-tile wall for the paired test of HW-003; HW-003 is dropped, so assert a full-speed charge stops at a one-tile wall there and drop the comment. The manifest example in `docs/plan/03-assets/pipeline.md` names the ruin gate painting; replace it with an entry that exists.
